## Part 1: Bibliographic Metadata

- emails: Array of author email addresses. Exclude editorial/journal contact emails (domains like nature.com, science.org, springer.com, elsevier.com, ieee.org, cell.com, pnas.org, aaas.org).
- corresponding_author: Array of names of corresponding authors (e.g. ["Alice Smith", "Bob Jones"]). Empty array if none.
